Urology Nomogram Cheat Sheet

Quick clinical reference for commonly used web-based urologic prediction tools.

Jump to disease area: Prostate Bladder Upper Tract (UTUC) Renal / RCC Testicular GCT Penile Stone Disease BPH / LUTS & Functional

Prostate Cancer

Screening / Biopsy Decision
PCPT Risk Calculator (v2)
Use: Estimate risk of any PCa and high-grade PCa pre-biopsy.
Inputs: Age, PSA, DRE, race, family history, prior biopsy.
https://www.riskcalc.org/PCPTRC/
ERSPC / SWOP Prostate Cancer Risk Calculators
Use: MRI-era risk of any PCa and clinically significant PCa; multiple modules (PSA-only, DRE, TRUS, MRI-based).
https://www.prostatecancer-riskcalculator.com/
MSKCC Pre-Biopsy Nomogram
Use: Probability of PCa and high-grade PCa on systematic biopsy.
https://www.mskcc.org/nomograms/prostate/biopsy_risk_dynamic
Stanford SPCC (MRI-based)
Use: Pre-biopsy probability of clinically significant PCa incorporating PI-RADS, PSA density, age, biopsy status.
https://med.stanford.edu/ucil/nomogram.html
UCLA PCRC-MRI Risk Calculator
Use: Risk of clinically significant PCa at MRI-guided biopsy (fusion or cognitive), combining clinical variables and MRI findings.
https://www.uclahealth.org/departments/urology/iuo/research/prostate-cancer/risk-calculator-mri-guided-biopsy-pcrc-mri
Localized Disease (Surgery / RT)
MSKCC Post-RP Recurrence Nomogram
Use: Time-dependent risk of biochemical recurrence after radical prostatectomy.
Inputs: Pathologic stage, grade, margins, PSA, etc.
https://www.mskcc.org/nomograms/prostate/pre_op
Tagged inactive — currently redirects to an unavailable page; remove after replacement is selected.
MSKCC EBRT / Brachytherapy Nomograms
Use: Freedom from progression after external beam RT or brachytherapy.
https://www.mskcc.org/nomograms/prostate
Tagged inactive — page currently returns 404; remove after replacement is selected.
Fox Chase Prostate Nomograms
Use: CAPRA & CAPRA-S, nodal invasion risk, BCR after RP, salvage RT outcomes, potency and continence recovery.
https://www.cancernomograms.com/nomograms/
Active Surveillance
Fox Chase MRI-Based AS Tools
Use: Risk of AS disqualification on repeat biopsy; risk of adverse pathology in AS candidates, integrating mpMRI and clinical data.
https://www.cancernomograms.com/nomograms/
Advanced Disease / PSMA
UCLA PSMA PET Upstaging Risk Calculator
Use: Predict probability that PSMA PET will upstage apparently localized prostate cancer to node-positive or metastatic disease.
https://www.uclahealth.org/departments/urology/iuo/research/prostate-cancer/psma-pet-upstaging-risk-calculator
UCLA LuPSMA Response Prediction Tool
Use: Predict PSA response and overall survival with ¹⁷⁷Lu-PSMA-617 therapy.
https://www.uclahealth.org/departments/urology/iuo/research/prostate-cancer/lupsma-response-prediction-tool

Bladder Cancer

NMIBC
EORTC NMIBC Risk Calculator
Use: Recurrence and progression risk estimates using classic EORTC tables.
https://www.eortc.be/tools/bladdercalculator/
CUETO BCG-Treated NMIBC Nomogram
Use: Recurrence and progression risk in BCG-treated NMIBC patients.
https://www.aeu.es/Cueto.html
Tagged inactive — remove after confirming replacement.
EAU NMIBC Risk (NMIBC.net)
Use: Updated European risk groups and progression estimates.
https://www.nmibc.net/
PROGRxN-BCa
Use: Quantifies risk of progression in non–muscle-invasive bladder cancer (NMIBC) using contemporary clinicopathologic variables; supports treatment and surveillance discussions.
https://progrxn.ca/Use_PROGRxN-BCa
Muscle-Invasive / Cystectomy
MSKCC Post-Cystectomy Nomogram
Use: 5-year recurrence and cancer-specific survival after radical cystectomy.
https://www.mskcc.org/nomograms/bladder/post_op
Fox Chase Bladder Nomograms
Use: Advanced disease probability at RC, post-RC recurrence and mortality, NAC+RC outcomes, peri-op mortality, competing-risk models.
https://www.cancernomograms.com/nomograms/

Renal / RCC

Post-Nephrectomy Prognosis
MSKCC RCC Recurrence Nomogram
Use: Risk of recurrence after nephrectomy for RCC.
https://www.mskcc.org/nomograms/renal/post_op
Fox Chase RCC Suite
Use: Leibovich, UISS, Karakiewicz, D-SSIGN, ASSURE and competing-risk survival models for RCC.
https://www.cancernomograms.com/nomograms/
Localized / Small Renal Mass
Small Renal Mass Risk Calculator (Fred Hutch)
Use: Treatment-specific mortality and complication risk for PN, RN, ablation, and active surveillance.
https://small-renal-mass-risk-calculator.fredhutch.org/
Kidney Upstaging Calculator (RiskCalc)
Use: Risk of pT3 upstaging for cT1 localized renal masses.
https://www.riskcalc.org/KidneyUpstaging/
RENAL Nephrometry Score (MDCalc / Fox Chase)
Use: Tumour complexity, malignancy and high-grade risk, and post-PN complication risk.
https://www.mdcalc.com/calc/3908/renal-nephrometry-score
Sarcomatoid RCC
Sarcomatoid RCC CSS Nomogram
Use: 3- and 5-year cancer-specific survival in sarcomatoid RCC (SEER-based).
https://yangtong.shinyapps.io/specific/
Sarcomatoid RCC OS Nomogram
Use: 3- and 5-year overall survival in sarcomatoid RCC.
https://yangtong.shinyapps.io/overall/

BPH / LUTS & Functional Urology

https://www.riskcalc.org/BenignProstaticHyperplasia/
